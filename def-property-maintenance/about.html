About Dylan & DEF Property Maintenance

DEF Property Maintenance is a working concept for Dylan's plans to expand into property maintenance. This page is intentionally conservative — it describes the idea behind the brand rather than making claims about experience, credentials, or history that haven't been confirmed.

The idea behind the brand

DEF Property Maintenance is designed as a scalable umbrella for property-services work — a structure that can hold several service lines side by side and let each one be introduced when it's ready, rather than requiring the whole site to be rebuilt each time.

This prototype exists to test that structure and its visual language with Dylan before any real content, credentials, or service commitments are added.

What this page deliberately does not include

To keep this prototype honest, the following are intentionally left out until Dylan confirms them:

Years in operation

No claim is made about how long the business has been running.

Certifications & licenses

No certifications, licenses, or insurance details are stated or implied.

Customer reviews

No testimonials or review counts are included — none exist for this prototype.

Team size & history

No claims about team size, past projects, or company history are made.

When this concept moves toward a real site, Dylan can supply accurate details for each of these, and this page can be updated accordingly.
