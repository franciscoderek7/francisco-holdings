Smart Security & Property Protection

Technology categories a modern property-protection company could assess and coordinate

This page catalogs the kinds of security and property-protection technology DEF could eventually assess, install, integrate, or coordinate as the business expands beyond maintenance. None of this is a claim that DEF currently sells, installs, or monitors any of it. Every category below carries its own honest status badge.

Important: DEF is not a licensed alarm company, a professional monitoring centre, a security guard company, or an insured/bonded/certified installer of any of the technology below — none of that is confirmed. Where a category is labeled Technology Showcase it means exactly that: an illustration of what a modern property-technology company could offer, not something DEF is currently selling.

Video Surveillance

Technology Showcase

Modern camera systems go well beyond a single fixed lens: night vision and wide-angle coverage, pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) units, and AI video analytics that can distinguish a person from a vehicle or a delivered package, watch defined activity zones, and record locally (NVR) or to the cloud. Any license-plate-aware camera capability would only ever be positioned where legally appropriate — this page makes no claim about what's permitted in any specific jurisdiction.

Could conceptually include

  • Fixed, wide-angle, and PTZ camera placement concepts
  • Night vision and low-light coverage
  • AI-assisted person / vehicle / package detection and activity zones
  • Local NVR or cloud-based recording options

Motion Detection

Technology Showcase

Passive infrared (PIR) and dual-technology sensors that detect movement indoors or out, including pet-friendly options tuned to ignore smaller animals, and smart analytics that try to reduce false alerts along a property's perimeter.

Could conceptually include

  • Indoor PIR and pet-friendly motion sensors
  • Outdoor-rated and dual-technology sensors
  • Smart analytics aimed at reducing false alerts
  • Perimeter-focused motion coverage

Ground / Perimeter Sensing

Assessment Required

Buried perimeter sensors, fence-mounted vibration sensors, driveway and gate-approach detection, infrared beam sensors, and radar-based sensing where it's an appropriate fit. This category is labeled Assessment Required rather than a showcase because ground/perimeter sensing is highly site-specific — terrain, property size, fencing, and local conditions all determine what (if anything) makes sense, so it can't be described generically the way an indoor sensor can.

Could conceptually include

  • Buried perimeter or fence-line vibration sensing
  • Driveway and gate-approach detection
  • Infrared beam sensors across an access point
  • Radar-based perimeter sensing where appropriate to the site

Door & Window Protection

Technology Showcase

Magnetic contact sensors and smart door/window sensors that report open, closed, or tampered-with states, extending to sliding doors, garage doors, and gates.

Could conceptually include

  • Magnetic contact sensors on doors and windows
  • Sliding-door, garage-door, and gate sensors
  • Tamper detection on sensors themselves

Glass Break Detection

Technology Showcase

Acoustic sensors that listen for the sound frequency of breaking glass, and vibration-based sensors that feel for the impact directly on the pane or frame.

Could conceptually include

  • Acoustic (sound-based) glass-break sensors
  • Vibration-based glass-break sensors mounted on the glass or frame

Smart Access Control

Technology Showcase

Smart locks, keypad entry, and mobile credentials that can grant temporary access to a contractor or family member and keep an audit log of who came and went, extending to garage/gate access and video intercoms. This is not presented as a regulated access-control service — it's a technology category, not a licensed offering.

Could conceptually include

  • Smart locks and keypad entry
  • Mobile credentials with temporary/contractor/family access
  • Access audit logs
  • Garage and gate access, video intercom / entry systems

Video Doorbells

Technology Showcase

Two-way audio, package detection, visitor alerts, remote answering from a phone, event recording, and integration with a broader smart-access setup.

Could conceptually include

  • Two-way audio and remote answering
  • Package and visitor detection with alerts
  • Event recording and smart-access integration

Smart Lighting Security

Technology Showcase

Motion-activated and automated exterior lighting along a driveway or pathway, occupancy- triggered lighting indoors, and vacation/presence-simulation lighting patterns while a property owner is away.

Could conceptually include

  • Motion-activated exterior and driveway/pathway lighting
  • Occupancy-triggered interior lighting
  • Vacation / presence-simulation lighting concepts
A Way To Think About It

The Security Layers model

A simple, seven-layer way to organize the categories above — from what's visible around a property to how an event is eventually handled. This is a conceptual model for explaining how the pieces fit together, not a description of a system DEF currently runs.

Visibility

Lighting, signage, and visible cameras — the layer that's simply seen from outside the property.

Perimeter

Fence lines, gates, driveway coverage, and ground/perimeter sensing around the outer edge of a property.

Entry

Doors, windows, locks, and door/window sensors — the points where someone actually gets in.

Interior

Indoor motion detection, glass-break sensors, and environmental sensors once inside the building.

Verification

Cameras, AI analytics, and correlating multiple events so an alert can be checked, not just triggered.

Automation

Lights, locks, alerts, and routines that respond automatically once something is detected and verified.

Response

Owner notification, and — only as future options, never a current promise — third-party monitoring or escalation services a homeowner could separately choose. DEF does not provide police or fire response, and does not operate professional monitoring.
