Proposed Services

Seven service categories, structured to grow

This page lays out the full proposed architecture: seven service categories, each with a clear status badge. The structure is intentionally modular — a category's status can be updated later (for example from "Coming Soon" to active) without a site redesign, because every card, badge, and detail section is built the same way.

How to read this page: every category below carries one of three statuses. None of the seven categories is presented as currently bookable. Statuses shown here are illustrative for this prototype — Dylan will decide the real status of each service before any of this goes live.

Property Maintenance

Availability TBC

The idea behind this category is a general, ongoing property-care service — the kind of recurring or on-call upkeep that keeps a property in good condition over time. It's intentionally described in general terms here; see the dedicated Property Care page for a fuller conceptual breakdown.

Could conceptually include

  • Routine walk-throughs or check-ins
  • General upkeep tasks as they arise
  • Coordinating minor issues before they grow

Seasonal Property Care

Coming Soon

A concept for work that follows the calendar — the kind of seasonal transition tasks properties often need. See the dedicated Seasonal Care page for the fuller conceptual breakdown, including cottage opening/closing style ideas.

Could conceptually include

  • Seasonal transition tasks around spring and fall
  • Preparing a property ahead of a change in weather
  • Cottage-style opening and closing concepts

Small Repairs

Potential Service

A possible future line for smaller, contained fix-it work — the sort of task that doesn't need a full renovation project but still needs doing. This is an idea under consideration, not a confirmed offering.

Could conceptually include

  • Minor fixes identified during a property visit
  • Small, discrete repair jobs
  • Bundling minor tasks into a single visit

Exterior Maintenance

Potential Service

A proposed category focused on the outside of a property, kept distinct from general indoor upkeep so it can be scoped, staffed, and priced separately if it's activated.

Could conceptually include

  • Exterior-facing upkeep tasks
  • Grounds and structure-adjacent maintenance concepts
  • Work that depends on outdoor conditions

Cottage / Property Services

Coming Soon

A concept for secondary, recreational, or vacation-style properties, which often have different needs than a primary residence — this category is planned as a separate line for that reason.

Could conceptually include

  • Care concepts specific to secondary properties
  • Coordination for properties visited less frequently
  • Overlap with seasonal transition concepts

Turnover Services

Potential Service

A possible category for the work involved in turning a property around between occupancies — relevant to rental or managed-property contexts if the business expands that direction.

Could conceptually include

  • Between-occupancy turnaround concepts
  • Coordinated readiness checks for a property
  • Timing turnaround work around occupancy changes

Vendor Coordination

Availability TBC

A conceptual layer where DEF Property Maintenance could coordinate specialist trades or third-party vendors on a job, rather than performing every task directly. Whether and how this operates is still to be confirmed.

Could conceptually include

  • Coordinating outside specialists for a job
  • Acting as a single point of contact across vendors
  • Scheduling handoffs between trades

This page is structured so that any of the seven categories above can be activated later — updating a badge and adding real details — without a site redesign. The card grid, status-badge system, and detail-section layout are all reusable as-is.
