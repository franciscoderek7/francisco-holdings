Concept & Prototype

A scalable property-services brand, built to grow one service line at a time.

DEF Property Maintenance is a concept for organizing Dylan's broader property-maintenance business expansion. This prototype shows how a full family of services could be presented, activated, and scheduled — without committing to anything that isn't confirmed yet.

How this prototype is structured

  • Every service category carries an honest status badge
  • No prices, phone numbers, or credentials are invented
  • New service lines can be switched on without a redesign
  • All forms are demo-only — nothing is sent or stored
