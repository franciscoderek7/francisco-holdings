About Dylan

An independent business, built from hands-on experience

Dylan has worked in the window-coverings field and is now putting that experience toward building his own independent, long-term business: Northern Forge Windows, Blinds & Doors — recently expanded from a blinds-only concept to also cover windows and doors.

The idea behind Northern Forge

Northern Forge Windows, Blinds & Doors is a concept for a home-openings business that Dylan is building on his own — separate and independent from any employer or business he has worked for or with in the past. It is not a rebrand, purchase, or continuation of any existing company.

The idea is straightforward: bring hands-on, in-field experience with blinds, shades, shutters, and motorization to a business built around clear communication, careful measurement, and installs meant to hold up over time — the kind of durability implied by the “forge” in the name. The scope has since expanded beyond window coverings alone to include windows and doors, reflecting a broader vision for the business.

This site is an early, non-functional prototype meant to gather feedback on direction, tone, and structure before any real business details, pricing, or public launch are finalized.

What Dylan brings to a project

Field experience

Time spent working directly in the window-coverings field, ahead of starting an independent business with an expanded windows-and-doors scope.

Straightforward process

A plan to keep consultations, measurements, and installs clear and easy to follow, start to finish — across windows, blinds, shades, shutters, doors, garage doors, and motorization.

Long-term intent

Northern Forge is being built as an independent, long-term business — not a side project or a quick flip.
