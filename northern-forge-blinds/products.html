Windows

Northern Forge’s expanded scope now includes window planning alongside window coverings — replacement, energy-efficient, modern, custom, residential, and specialty windows, described below as conceptual categories.

Replacement Windows

For homes where the existing window frame is dated, drafty, or difficult to operate — full or insert replacements modernize an opening without changing the exterior structure.

Energy-Efficient Windows

Window designs and glazing approaches aimed at reducing drafts and heat transfer, described only conceptually here — no specific technical ratings or performance claims until real product lines are confirmed.

Modern Windows

Clean-lined, larger-glass window styles suited to contemporary builds and renovations, often paired with minimal frame sightlines.

Custom Window Solutions

Non-standard shapes, sizes, or openings — bay, angled, oversized, or awkward-fit windows — planned around an accurate on-site measurement.

Residential Windows

Standard single-family and multi-unit residential window planning, from single-room swaps to whole-home projects.

Specialty Windows

Arched, circular, transom, or other architectural window shapes that go beyond a standard rectangular opening.

Blinds

Horizontal and vertical slat blinds tilt open and closed for precise, adjustable light control, and read as a clean, architectural line on a window.

Key considerations

  • Slat material and finish (aluminum, faux-wood, wood) affect durability and look.
  • Light gap between slats is a trade-off against full privacy when closed.
  • Moisture-prone rooms (bathrooms, kitchens) usually favor a non-wood slat.
  • Cleaning and dusting is an ongoing part of upkeep with slatted styles.
Start a Consultation About Blinds

Shades

Roller, cellular (honeycomb), and Roman shades use continuous fabric instead of slats, giving a softer look and a wide range of opacities.

Key considerations

  • Opacity ranges from sheer, to light-filtering, to full blackout.
  • Cellular shades can add a measure of insulation at the window.
  • Cordless and motorized lift options address child-safety concerns.
  • Fabric shades are generally simpler to keep clean than slatted styles.
Start a Consultation About Shades

Shutters

Solid, louvered interior shutter panels are the most substantial and permanent of the soft-treatment categories, and are typically framed to fit an exact window opening.

Key considerations

  • Panels are custom-fit to the window frame, so accurate measurement matters most here.
  • Material choice (wood vs. composite) matters more in humid rooms.
  • Shutters generally have a longer lead time than soft window treatments.
  • As a fixed, built-in feature, shutters are often considered a longer-term investment.

Replacement Doors

Motorization

Motorization adds motor-driven operation to blinds, shades, or shutters, controlled by remote, wall switch, or app — rather than a cord or wand.

Key considerations

  • Power source: rechargeable/battery units versus hardwired options.
  • Motorization can often be retrofitted onto compatible existing treatments, or built into a new install.
  • App and schedule-based control can pair with a broader smart-home setup — see the Technology Showcase below.
  • Typically the highest-cost option of the window-covering categories; scope depends on the number of windows.
