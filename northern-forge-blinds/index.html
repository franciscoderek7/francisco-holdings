Why build it this way

Traditional website vs. AI-powered website

This concept swaps the old browse-and-call pattern for a guided, lead-capturing flow. The Concierge and Product Finder shown here are simulated demos, not live AI services.

The old pattern

A traditional website

  1. 1Visitor lands on the homepage and has to guess where to click.
  2. 2They read through several pages trying to match their project to a category.
  3. 3Eventually they find a phone number and have to call and explain everything from scratch.
  4. 4If they don’t call right away, the visit usually leads nowhere.
VS
This concept (demo)

An AI-guided website

  1. 1Visitor arrives and is invited straight into the Concierge (demo) or the Product Finder (demo).
  2. 2A few guided questions surface what they actually need — room, light control, privacy, style, budget range.
  3. 3The tool suggests a starting product direction, explains the reasoning, and captures their contact details as a lead.
  4. 4That lead routes straight into a real consultation or quote request — no cold call required.

Try the Concierge on the consultation page, or the Product Finder on the products page.
