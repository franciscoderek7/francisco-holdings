A different starting point

Traditional website vs. this AI-concierge concept

Same goal — get you toward the right window coverings — but this prototype explores a different path to get there.

Typical business website

  1. Visitor lands on the homepage and has to guess where to click.
  2. Clicks through several pages looking for the right product or answer.
  3. Eventually finds a phone number or contact form, often near the bottom.
  4. Calls or emails cold, repeating context that's already on the site.

This concept

  1. Visitor arrives and an AI concierge asks what they're working on.
  2. It asks a few guided questions about light, privacy, style and budget direction.
  3. It summarizes a recommended direction in plain language.
  4. It hands off straight into a short consultation form, already carrying what it learned.
